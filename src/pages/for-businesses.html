Questions

Is it really free to claim my listing?

Yes. Claiming and keeping your listing up to date is free on the Basic plan, for as long as you like. Paid plans are optional.

How long does checking take?

We check claims, events, classes and offers within about 2 working days, and email you either way. Paid upgrades switch on as soon as your payment succeeds.

What is the difference between an event and a class?

An event happens on a set date, like a quiz night or a market, and leaves What’s On once it’s over. A class runs every week, like a dance class or a football session, and keeps the same page for as long as it runs.

Can I manage more than one business?

Yes. Add or claim each business from the same account. Plans are per listing.
