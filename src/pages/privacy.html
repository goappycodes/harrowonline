3. Business claims and documents

When you claim a listing we try the simplest check first: an email address on the business’s own website domain, or a phone call to the listed business number. If neither works, we may ask you to upload a document such as a utility bill, business rates bill or Companies House record.

  • Uploaded documents are private. Only Harrow Online staff who review claims can see them. They are never shown on the Hub.
  • We delete claim documents [90 days, to confirm] after we approve or reject the claim.
  • We keep a short record of the decision (who claimed, when, and the outcome) for as long as the listing exists.

4. Newsletter (Beehiiv)

Our newsletters are sent through Beehiiv. When you sign up on the Hub, including by unlocking a subscriber-only offer or ticking the newsletter box when you register, we send your email address, your edition choices and the sign-up source to Beehiiv.

To unlock a subscriber-only offer we check your email against our subscriber list. We only tell you whether the offer is unlocked; we never reveal whether any other email address is subscribed. If you unlock an offer we set a cookie so you aren’t asked again for 30 days.

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. You can also email us to be removed.

5. Payments (Stripe)

Hub plans, newsletter sponsor slots and featured listings are paid through Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe’s own secure pages. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us whether a payment worked and gives us the details we need for invoices. You can update your card or cancel through the Stripe customer portal.

7. Businesses listed on the Hub

Many listings were created from open data published by the Overture Maps Foundation. They contain business information such as name, address, phone number and website. Where a business is run by a sole trader, some of this may be personal information. We use it under our legitimate interest in publishing a useful local directory. See Data sources for details.

Imported listings are hidden from search engines until the business claims and completes them. If you run a listed business and want details corrected, or the listing removed, use the correction and removal form. We aim to act within five working days.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that’s wrong
  • delete your information, where we don’t need to keep it
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to us using it
  • send your information to you or another organisation in a common format

Where we rely on consent, such as for the newsletter, you can withdraw it at any time. We reply to requests within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
