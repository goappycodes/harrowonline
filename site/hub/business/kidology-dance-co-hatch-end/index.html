About Kidology Dance Co

Kidology Dance Co (KDC) is a dance and performing arts school at Harrow Arts Centre in Hatch End, with classes for children and young people from toddlers to teenagers. It was founded in 2010 by Nicky Rutter and Lauren O’Connell.

Classes cover street dance, hip-hop, breakdance, contemporary and musical theatre, helping young dancers build their skills and confidence and make friends. Students have performed at Move It, the UK Streetdance Championships and the London New Year’s Day Parade, and everyone can take part in the annual showcase.

Classes run by half term and must be booked in advance. A free trial class is available: get in touch to arrange one. KDC also runs dance workshops for schools and community groups, including after-school and breakfast clubs, PE lessons and GCSE dance support.
