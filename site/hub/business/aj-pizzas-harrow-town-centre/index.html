About AJ Pizza's

AJ Pizza’s is a pizza takeaway on Headstone Road, a short walk from Harrow town centre. Alongside favourites such as Margherita and pepperoni, the menu has speciality pizzas including Peri Peri Chicken, Chicken Tikka, Meat Feast and AJ’s Special, plus vegetarian choices such as Paneer Tikka and Veg Supreme.

Sides include chicken wings, garlic bread, mozzarella sticks and curly fries, with desserts, soft drinks and bubble tea. Order for collection or home delivery, with meal deals for one, for families and for groups.

Open every day from 12pm to 11pm. You can order online at ajpizzas.co.uk.
