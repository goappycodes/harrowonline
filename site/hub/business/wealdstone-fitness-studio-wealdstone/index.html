About Wealdstone Fitness Studio

An independent studio for people who don't like big gyms. Classes are capped at 12, so coaches know your name and your goals.

We run strength, spin, yoga and weekend bootcamps, plus beginner courses every month. Showers, lockers and free towels included.

New members start with a free one-to-one session to set goals, and every class has an option for beginners.
