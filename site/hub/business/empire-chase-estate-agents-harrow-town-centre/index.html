About Empire Chase Estate Agents

Empire Chase is an independent estate and letting agency on Peterborough Road in Harrow Town Centre, specialising in residential sales, lettings and property management. It was founded in 2012 and says it now manages more than 650 properties.

The team works with homeowners who want to sell, buyers looking for their next home, tenants, and landlords who need help finding tenants or managing their properties. Homes are marketed with professional photography, digital marketing and social media, and the agency has won a number of industry awards for its sales and lettings work.

For investors, developers and landlords, Empire Chase also offers block management, Build to Rent services, property investment and asset management, and property market research. It covers Harrow, Harrow on the Hill, Kenton, Wealdstone, Pinner, Wembley, Kingsbury, North Harrow, South Harrow and nearby areas.

Property management enquiries: propertymanagement@empirechase.co.uk. Search homes for sale and to rent at empirechase.co.uk.
