About Meeting Palace

Meeting Palace is a Sri Lankan restaurant on the High Street in Wealdstone, serving traditional Sri Lankan dishes. It has traded under the Meeting Palace name since May 2006 and is part of Wealdstone’s local food scene.

Come for lunch, an evening meal or a get-together with family and friends. The restaurant also caters for functions and private parties.
