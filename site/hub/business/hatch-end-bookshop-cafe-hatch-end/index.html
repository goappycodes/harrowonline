About Hatch End Bookshop & Café

A small independent bookshop with a café at the back. We stock new fiction, local history and a big children's section, plus a rotating shelf of second-hand finds.

Coffee is roasted in North London, cakes are baked locally, and story time for under-6s runs every Saturday morning.

We order any book in print within two days, host monthly author evenings and run a reading group on the first Thursday of the month.
