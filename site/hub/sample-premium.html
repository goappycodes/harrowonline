About Hill Street Kitchen

A family-run restaurant at the top of Harrow on the Hill, cooking seasonal British food with produce from farms in Hertfordshire and Buckinghamshire.

Come for long lunches, early dinners before a show at the school, or our Sunday roast, which Harrow Online readers voted the best in the borough this year. The heated garden terrace is open all year, and dogs are welcome on it.

We have a private room for up to 24 guests, step-free access from the High Street, and a children’s menu every day.
