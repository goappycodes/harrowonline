About this class

A friendly, hands-on coding club where children build their own games, animations and quizzes. Younger coders start with Scratch; older children move on to Python and make a simple game they can take home and keep improving.

Groups are small (up to 10 children per tutor) and every session ends with a show and tell. Laptops are provided, so children don’t need to bring anything.

What to know

  • Children aged 8 and 9 need an adult to collect them
  • The library is step-free, with toilets on the ground floor
  • Bring a water bottle and a snack for the break
