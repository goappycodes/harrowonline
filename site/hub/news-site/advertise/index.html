1. Hub memberships

List your business on Harrow Online Hub

The Hub is Harrow’s local business directory, with Local Offers and What’s On. Claiming your listing is free. Paid plans put you at the top of your category, add offers and events, and feature you in the newsletter.

  • Basic, free: verified listing, hours, description and a photo
  • Enhanced, £19 a month: more photos, Local Offers and events
  • Premium, £49 a month: featured at the top of your category and on the Hub homepage
  • Local Partner, £149 a month: Premium plus a monthly newsletter feature, Facebook Group post and a quarterly spotlight article

3. Facebook Group sponsorship

Sponsor our Harrow Facebook Group

Our Facebook Group is where Harrow residents share news, ask for recommendations and talk about what’s happening locally. Sponsors get a pinned, clearly labelled post from our team, written to fit the group.

  • A sponsored post written with you and approved before it goes up
  • Pinned to the top of the group for the agreed period
  • Included every month in the Local Partner plan
