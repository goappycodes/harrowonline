Independent shops back late-night Christmas shopping on Harrow on the Hill

By Harrow Online reporter · Wednesday 11 November 2026 · 3 min read

Harrow on the Hill high street. Image placeholder.

Independent businesses on Harrow on the Hill will stay open until 9pm every Thursday in December, after traders agreed a joint plan to bring more evening shoppers to the area.

The late openings start on Thursday 3 December and run until Christmas Eve, with lights on the high street and a small market on the green on the first two evenings.

Ad slot (Ad Inserter, unchanged)

Restaurant owners say evening trade has been slow since the summer, and hope that a shared effort will show customers the hill is open after six.

Hill Street Kitchen, one of the businesses taking part, will run a set Christmas menu on the late nights. Letting agents and other services on the hill have also agreed to keep their offices open to support the scheme.

Traders have asked the council to look at evening parking on the nights involved.

Traders say they will review the scheme in January and may repeat it for the summer holidays if it works.
