Support independent local journalism

Harrow Online is free to read and always will be. Readers who give a few pounds a month help us keep reporting on the council, schools, crime, planning and the people of Harrow.

Why it matters

Local papers have closed across London. In many boroughs nobody sits in council meetings any more, or asks questions when a planning decision goes through. Harrow Online does that work every day, for free.

Advertising pays some of the bills, but not all of them. Your support pays for reporting time: going to meetings, checking facts and following stories that matter to Harrow, not just the ones that get clicks.

You’ll pay on a secure Stripe page. No account needed. Cancel any time.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Just choose an amount and pay on the Stripe page. You don’t need a Harrow Online or Hub login.

How do I change or cancel my support?

Stripe emails you a receipt each month with a link to manage your support. Use it to update your card, change the amount or cancel. It takes a minute and there’s no notice period.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, which is used by millions of businesses. We never see or store your card details.

Does supporting change what I see?

No. Harrow Online stays free for everyone. Supporters keep it that way, and nobody gets a say over what we report.

Why trust us

  • Independent local journalism, focused only on Harrow.
  • Regulated by IMPRESS, the independent press regulator, with a public complaints procedure.
  • Member of the Independent Community News Network.
  • Free to read, with no paywall.

Where your money goes

Reporting time on Harrow stories: council and planning meetings, courts, schools and community news.
