Questions

Is claiming really free?

Yes. Claiming puts you on the Basic plan, which is free for as long as you like. You get a verified listing, your business details and opening hours, one photograph, and you can keep the listing up to date yourself.

What’s the difference between Basic and Featured?

Basic gets your business listed and verified. Featured puts you ahead of other businesses in your category and in search, gives you featured positions around the Hub, and lets you add more photographs, social media links, Local Offers, and events and classes.

How long does it take to go live?

Claims are checked by our team, usually within 2 working days, and we email you either way. That’s the only approval step. Once your listing is yours, paid upgrades go live the moment your payment succeeds. Offers and events are checked before they appear.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes. Switch in the Stripe customer portal, linked from your account. Annual plans cost the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free. Any change is prorated, so you only pay for what you use.

What happens if I downgrade or cancel?

Your plan runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for, then your listing drops to Basic. We keep all your data. Extra photos, social media links, offers and featured spots are hidden, not deleted, so everything comes back if you upgrade again.

What if a payment fails?

Stripe tries again over the following days and emails you so you can update your card. If the last try fails, your listing drops to Basic with all your data kept.

Do you take discount codes?

Yes. If we’ve given you a code, enter it on the Stripe Checkout page. Codes work on Hub plans only.

What does a Local Partner get?

A featured presence on the Hub, plus one newsletter feature and one Harrow Online Facebook Group post each month, a sponsored social media post or collaboration, and a sponsored spotlight article on harrowonline.org each quarter. Our team writes and schedules them with you.

Can I run an event without a paid plan?

Schools, clubs and charities can open a free organiser account and submit events and classes without claiming a listing. Create a free account.
