About this event

Harrow’s Christmas lights go on for the season with an evening of free family fun along St Ann’s Road. Bring the children for the lantern parade, warm up with food from local traders and stay for the big countdown at 6pm.

What’s on

  • 4pm: food and craft stalls open, with face painting for children
  • 4.30pm: school choirs and local bands on the main stage
  • 5.30pm: lantern parade from St George’s Shopping Centre
  • 6pm: countdown and lights switch-on
  • 6.15pm to 8pm: live music and late opening in town centre shops

Getting there

Harrow-on-the-Hill station (Metropolitan line) and Harrow bus station are a two-minute walk away. St Ann’s Road is closed to traffic from 2pm to 9pm. The nearest car parks fill up early, so public transport is best.
