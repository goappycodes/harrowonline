About Pinner Village Florist

The shop front on Bridge Street with buckets of seasonal flowers

An independent florist on Bridge Street, in the heart of Pinner village, since 2009. We make hand-tied bouquets, wedding and event flowers, funeral tributes and houseplant gifts, with British-grown stems whenever they’re in season.

Order in the shop or by phone. We deliver across Pinner, Hatch End, Northwood and Harrow, with same-day delivery on orders placed before 12pm, Monday to Saturday.
